How I can help

Every plan starts with a thorough assessment. Treatment is tailored to you, and you'll always leave with something you can do yourself.

Joint and back pain

Arthritis, back and neck pain, stiff or painful joints.

Nerve pain

Sciatica, trapped nerves, pins and needles or numbness in the arms and legs.

Injuries

Sprains, strains, frozen shoulder, tendon problems, knee and foot injuries.

Rehabilitation

Getting moving again after a stroke, a neurological condition, illness or an operation.

Headaches

Tension headaches linked to the neck, posture and stress.

Wellbeing

Sleep, anxiety and stress management, bladder control, and lowering heart-health risks.
