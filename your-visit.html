Your visit and fees

Your first appointment is thorough, unhurried and focused on you.

What to expect

  1. A conversation first. We talk about your problem to check physiotherapy is right for you.
  2. Your history. About an hour together, covering your health, daily activities, symptoms and how they change.
  3. A physical examination. Some specific tests and movements to find what's causing the problem.
  4. Diagnosis and first treatment. I explain what's going on, give a treatment to ease symptoms, and at least one exercise or stretch to start at home.
  5. Your plan. We discuss options and what to expect in the short, medium and long term.

Fees

£80

Initial assessment, about one hour, with some treatment if time allows.

Follow-up sessions are tailored to you: from half an hour to an hour, depending on what's needed. The cost depends on the treatment plan we agree, and is always discussed and agreed before you commit. Some examples:

  • Acupuncture £50
  • Spinal mobilisation £30
  • Exercise prescription £20

Payment is by bank transfer, using a link I'll send you.
